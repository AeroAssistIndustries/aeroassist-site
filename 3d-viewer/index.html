Is the $5,000 deposit refundable?

No. The deposit starts the build of your aircraft (for the Hybrid Flagship, it holds your place in the first production run), so it's non-refundable. It counts toward the price.

When do I pay the rest?

The balance is due before your aircraft ships. We confirm the amount, your build slot and the delivery window in writing after your deposit.

We're an agency. Can we pay by purchase order or grant?

Yes. Agencies usually buy through a purchase order, a bid or a grant. Talk to our team and we'll work with your procurement rules and provide NDAA documentation for grant files.

Can I see one fly first?

Yes. Book a demo at our Phoenix shop, or ask us to bring one to you.

Do I need an FAA waiver?

Flying beyond visual line of sight for first-responder programs generally needs FAA approval for your agency. We help you prepare; approval is between your agency and the FAA.

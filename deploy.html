Program tiers

Three ways to start, one way to grow.

Every program is quoted to your sector, so there is no price list. These are the three shapes most programs take, and each one grows into the next without replacing what you bought.

FOR ONE AGENCY

Responder

A single agency with one aircraft and dock, live streaming to dispatch.

  • XR-1 aircraft and one dock
  • Live video to dispatch
  • Operator training and certification
  • Standard support
FOR A DEPARTMENT

Command

A multi-site department running a docked network.

  • Docked network across districts
  • Incident-command console
  • Program analytics and public reporting
  • Priority support
FOR A COUNTY OR REGION

Regional

County-wide coverage shared across agencies.

  • XR-3 wide-area response
  • Cross-agency integration and mutual aid
  • Dedicated program team
  • Everything in Command

Deployment roadmap

From first call to first launch, in about ninety days.

Click through the phases. This is the plan we run with an agency starting from zero.

Sector study

Days 1–14

—

    Training and certification

    Your people run it. We make sure they can.

    01

    Operator course

    Console operation, tasking logic, payload use and scene discipline. Runs on the same interface as the live system, including failure scenarios.

    02

    Supervisor and policy

    Approval workflows, what gets recorded, retention, release, and the decisions a supervisor owns during an incident.

    03

    Maintenance and dock

    Field-level servicing, dock health, fuel handling and the checks that keep an airframe available instead of grounded.

    04

    Recurrent and audit

    Scheduled recurrency, log review, and an annual program audit against your own policy and the FAA authorities you hold.

    PHOENIX, ARIZONA
    American built. Arizona proud. Mission first.

    AeroAssist designs, fabricates and builds its aircraft in Phoenix, Arizona, for the agencies that protect American communities, under NDAA §848 supply-chain rules.

    BUILT IN THE USA
    NDAA §848 COMPLIANT
    ARIZONA · EST. 2022

    Airframes are designed, fabricated and assembled in Phoenix, Arizona. A limited number of components are sourced abroad where no qualifying domestic supplier exists. NDAA compliance refers to Section 848 of the FY2020 National Defense Authorization Act.

    REGULATORY AND COMPLIANCE

    • NDAA §848CompliantSecure supply chain: no covered foreign-made drone components, so agencies can buy with federal and grant funds.
    • FAAPart 107 · COA · BVLOSPrograms fly under Part 107, a COA and a BVLOS waiver. We do the filing work with your agency.
    • FCCCertifiedRadio and electronics are FCC certified for sale and use in the United States.
    • CECE markedConforms with the applicable EU safety, health and environmental requirements.

    Compliance status is shown as it stands today and is updated as certifications are granted.

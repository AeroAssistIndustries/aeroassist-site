A mission, start to finish

Four steps, every call, without a pilot on the roof.

Call drops

Dispatch tasks the nearest aircraft straight from the incident record. No crew to mobilize, no truck to roll.

Aircraft launches

The dock opens and the aircraft is airborne in seconds, streaming to dispatch from the moment it clears the pad.

Overwatch holds

It arrives ahead of ground units and stays on station, giving incident command a continuous look at the scene.

Returns and resets

On clear, the aircraft flies home, re-docks, refuels and charges itself, ready before the next call comes in.

Fits your stack

It has to work with the systems dispatch already lives in.

A console nobody opens is worthless. AeroAssist is built to push into the tools your people already have on screen.

CAD

Calls flow in from computer-aided dispatch so tasking happens on the incident record, not in a second system.

RMS and evidence

Video, snapshots and flight logs export to records and digital evidence management with the incident number attached.

Live video to the field

Streams to mobile data terminals and phones so the responding unit sees the scene on the way, not after.

Real-time crime center

Feeds into RTCC video walls alongside fixed cameras, with the aircraft as one more movable source.

Mutual aid

Share a live feed with a neighboring agency or fire command for the duration of an incident, then revoke it.

Reporting and audit

Every flight, operator, payload activation and video access is logged and exportable for oversight.

PHOENIX, ARIZONA
American built. Arizona proud. Mission first.

AeroAssist designs, fabricates and builds its aircraft in Phoenix, Arizona, for the agencies that protect American communities, under NDAA §848 supply-chain rules.

BUILT IN THE USA
NDAA §848 COMPLIANT
ARIZONA · EST. 2022

Airframes are designed, fabricated and assembled in Phoenix, Arizona. A limited number of components are sourced abroad where no qualifying domestic supplier exists. NDAA compliance refers to Section 848 of the FY2020 National Defense Authorization Act.

REGULATORY AND COMPLIANCE

  • NDAA §848CompliantSecure supply chain: no covered foreign-made drone components, so agencies can buy with federal and grant funds.
  • FAAPart 107 · COA · BVLOSPrograms fly under Part 107, a COA and a BVLOS waiver. We do the filing work with your agency.
  • FCCCertifiedRadio and electronics are FCC certified for sale and use in the United States.
  • CECE markedConforms with the applicable EU safety, health and environmental requirements.

Compliance status is shown as it stands today and is updated as certifications are granted.

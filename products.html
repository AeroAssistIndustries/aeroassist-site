How ordering works

From deposit to first flight.

Every aircraft is built to order in our Phoenix shop. The deposit is what starts the build.

  1. Reserve with $5,000

    Choose your aircraft and pay a $5,000 deposit per aircraft. It's non-refundable and starts your build. For XR-3 it holds your place in the first production run.

  2. Configure and confirm

    We confirm your sensors and options, your build slot and your delivery window in writing. Pricing for docks, spares and service is quoted here.

  3. Build and acceptance

    We build, test and flight-check your aircraft in Phoenix. The balance is due before it ships TBC. You're welcome to see it fly first.

  4. Delivery and onboarding

    We deliver, help you set up and train your first operators TBC. Your warranty starts on delivery.

Included with every aircraft

  • The aircraft, built, flight-tested and serialized in Phoenix
  • Standard sensor payload for the model, configured to your order
  • Flight batteries, charger and transport case TBC
  • Command center software access for dispatch, live video and logs TBC
  • 12-month limited warranty and phone and email support from our team

Quoted separately

  • Dock stations, installation and site survey
  • Extra batteries, propellers and spare parts
  • Extended coverage and service plans
  • Sales tax, shipping and on-site training beyond onboarding
Warranty and support

Built here, fixed here.

Because we build every aircraft ourselves, repairs come back to the people who made it, in Phoenix, not to an overseas depot.

What's covered DRAFT

  • 12 months from delivery against defects in materials and workmanship, on the aircraft and AeroAssist-built equipment.
  • Flight batteries: 12 months or TBC charge cycles, whichever comes first.
  • Hybrid generator (XR-2, XR-3): 12 months or TBC run hours, whichever comes first.
  • Our choice of remedy: repair, replacement or refund of the affected part.
  • Repairs are covered for 90 days or the rest of the original warranty, whichever is longer.
  • We pay shipping both ways for covered claims in the United States.

What isn't

  • Crashes, impacts and collisions, unless a covered defect caused them
  • Misuse, unauthorized modifications or repairs, and removed serial numbers
  • Propellers and other wear items, and normal cosmetic wear
  • Damage from fire, water or conditions outside the published operating limits
  • Aircraft bought outside the United States or from unauthorized sellers

Making a claim: email info@aeroassist.us with the serial number, what happened and the flight log. We'll send a return authorization and a prepaid label.

This is a summary. The written limited warranty provided with your order governs. Extended coverage, including crash replacement, can be added when you order: ask us.

Questions

Before you order.

Is the $5,000 deposit refundable?

No. The deposit starts the build of your aircraft (or holds your place in the XR-3 production run), so it's non-refundable. It counts toward the price of the aircraft.

When do I pay the rest?

The balance is due before your aircraft ships TBC. We confirm the amount, the build slot and the delivery window in writing after your deposit.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the model and the order book. We confirm your delivery window when we schedule your build TBC. XR-3 is a pre-order: deposits hold a place in the first production run.

We're an agency and pay by purchase order. Can we still order?

Yes. Agencies usually buy through a purchase order, a bid or a grant rather than a card deposit. Use the enterprise and government form and we'll work with your procurement rules.

Can federal grant money pay for these?

Our aircraft are built to the NDAA §848 supply-chain rule, which federally funded buyers increasingly require. We can provide documentation for grant files and sole-source justifications. Your grant terms decide what's eligible.

Do I need an FAA waiver to fly?

Flying beyond visual line of sight for drone-as-first-responder programs generally needs FAA approval for your agency. We can help you prepare, but approval is between your agency and the FAA.

Can I see one fly first?

Yes. Come to our shop at 4750 S 44th Pl, Suite E18, Phoenix, or ask us for a demonstration. Email info@aeroassist.us.

Who can buy?

We sell to public-safety agencies, enterprises and qualified operators in the United States. We may decline an order, in which case we refund any deposit in full.

Prices are in US dollars per aircraft, before sales tax and shipping, and may change before your order is confirmed in writing. A reservation becomes an order when we confirm it in writing. Specifications reflect target performance and may be refined as builds are finalized.
